MUVATASE ENGINEERING LIMITED
Muvatase Engineering Limited has been operational since 23 July
2009 when it was first registered. It was then operating as Muvatase
General Dealers enterprise until February 2020 when it became a
limited company after which it changed its name to Muvatase
General Dealers Limited. It then changed its name to Muvatase
Engineering on 3rd December 2024.
Muvatase Engineering Limited operates across a diverse range of
business areas, including but not limited to the following;
1.0 Supply of goods and services
2.0 Electrical Engineering related works such as;
 Installation and maintenance of electrical switchgears.
 Construction of substations up to 33kv voltage level.
 Construction of 33kv, 11kv and medium voltage overhead and
underground lines.
 Substation earthing and commissioning.
 Building of Mini Solar plants.
3.0 Consultancy services in electrical works
4.0 Construction projects
5.0 Transportation of goods
